## Adaptations

Native text and controls stay accessible without gestures. The player title owns
its vertical gesture so the rest of the page can scroll. Large text or a narrow
agenda window uses a chronological list; the normal phone uses time geometry.
Collection controls are part of its lazy container and remain reachable in short
windows. Dialogs show object/event detail and dismiss through Android Back.

Scene bodies and their tab selectors cap at 600 dp in wide windows. Experiment and
Understand keep separate scroll positions, so reading starts at the beginning.
